A simple experience over complex workflows

Forming a company quickly expands into EIN applications, registered agents, annual reports, corporate documents, equity, signatures, banking, and deadlines. Our customer wanted one membership product instead of a trail of disconnected providers and status updates.

Give the member one consistent experience while engineering every operation as an explicit, traceable workflow.

A portal organized around the company

The React and Next.js member portal keeps an active company at the centre. Guided formation, EIN tracking, compliance, beneficial ownership reporting, documents, equity, banking referrals, perks, billing, and AI-assisted guidance all live in one account.

The formation journey behaves like a guided questionnaire rather than a government form. Structured answers can drive filing preparation, registered-agent requirements, equity setup, signatures, and tax-election work downstream.

Documents as a product capability

A reusable generation pipeline collects and validates variables, merges maintained templates, renders PDFs, and stores the result for later access. Operating agreements, founder agreements, employment agreements, stock purchases, and other records can continue into electronic-signature workflows with webhook-driven status updates.

Built for asynchronous operations

A NestJS and PostgreSQL API uses BullMQ and Valkey queues for formation, EIN, compliance, AI, documents, and email. Slow or unreliable provider operations run away from the browser request, retry safely, and expose visible state to members and operators.

Stripe, object storage, Resend, OpenAI, filing providers, entity verification, signatures, and banking referrals sit behind service boundaries. Authentication, role-aware authorization, validation, rate limiting, restricted CORS, security headers, encrypted sensitive data, and fail-closed webhook verification protect the platform.

The Turborepo monorepo supports local Docker services and production deployment across Vercel and AWS ECS/Fargate, RDS, ElastiCache, ECR, and managed secrets, all provisioned with Terraform.

A foundation for the whole member journey

FoundryLane connects formation, compliance, documents, equity, signatures, billing, banking, and member services in one extensible system. Members get a clearer journey, operators gain visibility, and the product team can add providers and services without rebuilding the foundation.
