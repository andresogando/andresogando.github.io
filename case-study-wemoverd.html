A funnel built around real customer questions

The public site helps Punta Cana visitors understand vehicles, group capacity, services, rentals, sales, delivery, and pickup. Fleet categories, vehicle details, testimonials, use cases, and a focused contact form turn discovery into a clear next step.

Turning manual reservations into a workflow

Staff move through a guided booking flow for customer and licence details, identification images, vehicle selection, dates, pickup location, payment method, and assisting team member. Validation catches incomplete reservations early while live fleet data replaces separate lists.

Submission creates or matches the customer, uploads identification, reserves and assigns the vehicle, and preserves operational context. The completed booking produces a rental contract and a locally appropriate payment link through a Dominican provider.

The platform is not a digital form. It is the bridge between the front desk, fleet, contracts, and payment.

A focused tool for hotel partners

Authorized teams at partner hotels can arrange rentals for guests through protected, role-based views. Front-desk users see only reservation, contract, pickup, and return tools, while administrators manage accounts and vehicle assignments.

This lets WeMoveRD extend its service into premium hospitality settings while keeping control over fleet access and internal administration.

Supporting pickup through return

Staff can search reservations by name, phone, or email, then move confirmed bookings into active rentals. Returns capture mileage, fuel, condition, damage notes, observations, time, and images, classifying vehicles as ready, damaged, or unusable.

React and TypeScript applications share an Nx workspace. Azure serverless functions handle backend workflows, Cosmos DB stores assignments, and integrations connect rental management, payment, contracts, customers, vehicles, and live status.

One connected customer journey

A visitor discovers the fleet, a team member or hotel partner creates the reservation, and the system carries it through contract, payment, assignment, pickup, and documented return. WeMoveRD gained both a stronger digital presence and a more repeatable operation.
