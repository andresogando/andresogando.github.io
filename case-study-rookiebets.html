RookieBets · Responsible AI

Forecasts people can inspect, not just accept

A sports-broadcast-inspired product backed by controlled AI orchestration, deterministic rules, and honest probability language.

Starting with understanding

RookieBets organizes live scoreboards, markets, forecast history, and parlays around a simple question: what does a user need to understand today? Sport, date, status, and team filters reduce noise, while on-demand generation supports balanced and higher-risk profiles.

The product is explicitly informational. It does not accept bets or connect users to a sportsbook. Loading, unavailable, incomplete, and abstained states are first-class parts of the experience.

A platform, not a mockup

Three independently deployable applications divide responsibility: a React and TanStack Start web app, a Go and Chi API, and a Node.js intelligence service using LangGraph.js. The browser consumes a generated OpenAPI client; the Go service owns authoritative writes; and the intelligence service proposes but cannot publish.

Model output never becomes trusted product data without deterministic validation.

AI with traceability

One reusable forecasting graph is configured for MLB, NFL, parlay composition, criticism, and explanation. It loads context, evaluates data quality, computes probabilities, builds candidates, runs critic review, validates structured output, and submits proposals to the API.

The workflow can abstain when data is incomplete. Forecasts are immutable, corrections are linked revisions, every result records its agent version, internal routes require authentication, and idempotency keys protect repeated proposals.

Responsible probability communication

Cards separate estimated probability, market-implied probability, and edge. Deterministic logic handles American and decimal odds, vig removal, market outcomes, data freshness, and outliers. Correlated combinations are rejected or clearly identified as experimental when dependency cannot be priced correctly.

The language follows the same discipline: forecast, estimate, experimental, and model abstained replace claims such as guaranteed or risk-free.

Built for confident iteration

A fixture-first path runs the complete product locally without paid data, databases, or model credentials. Automated tests cover interfaces, Go domain logic, AI routing, accessibility, probability behaviour, parlays, schemas, and integration boundaries.

New leagues, providers, and model versions can be introduced while preserving the transparency and controlled boundaries that make the product trustworthy.
