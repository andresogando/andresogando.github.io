RMJ Transportation · Local growth

From phone-only bookings to a 24/7 lead engine

A bilingual, accessible, search-first digital front door built around the trust parents need when choosing school transportation.

Designing around safety and community

RMJ had earned trust on the road as a family-owned business with 7D-certified, background-checked drivers, but its digital presence remained phone-first. We built the site in English and Spanish from the start, with language choice persisting across sessions.

Actual fleet imagery and clear proof points—family-owned, certified, checked, local, and on time—replace generic corporate claims. Reviews and a linked map let families verify the business for themselves.

Making the business findable

Geo-targeted metadata, social cards, canonical URLs, hreflang, and structured data establish a local search foundation. Service, FAQ, breadcrumb, website, and local-business schemas help search engines understand exactly what RMJ offers and where.

FAQ answers target the questions parents actually ask about cities served and school transportation in Lawrence, Massachusetts. Image loading and CSS-first rendering keep the experience fast.

For a local service business, the website is the first mile of every route.

A contact form for real operations

The form captures what RMJ needs to quote a route: contact details, inquiry type, school, pickup, drop-off, and message. Browser validation catches incomplete or malformed submissions before the server.

A serverless handler uses Resend with a zero-configuration fallback. Parents receive immediate confirmation while RMJ gets a formatted route inquiry. The same channel supports driver applications, and mobile visitors retain persistent click-to-call access.

Mobile and accessible by default

The static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site loads quickly on older devices and weak connections. Large touch targets, a mobile drawer, skip links, landmarks, semantic headings, ARIA labels, and visible keyboard focus make the experience usable while multitasking or with assistive technology.

A digital presence that matches the reputation

Parents searching for school transportation can now find RMJ, understand its credentials, review local proof, and request a route at any time. The website extends word-of-mouth trust into a bilingual lead-generation system.
